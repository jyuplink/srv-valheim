Live on Fjordnite now

Emotes are the buttons. Vanilla Valheim has no mod menu, so the server watches your emotes. Sit puts your inventory away into chests that already hold those items. Wave opens your backpack. Use the emote wheel, type /sit or /wave in chat, or bind a key with /bind <key> Wave.

Sit does two jobs. Sitting also counts as a yes vote to skip the night, so voting to sleep beside your chests puts your things away too. Nothing is lost; it only fills chests that already hold that item.
